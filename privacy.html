Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Babaji Kehnde Ne Live is an application intended to help the operator of the Babaji Kehnde Ne YouTube channel manage scheduled livestreams.

Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access YouTube channel information and livestream resources through the YouTube Data API.

How Information Is Used

Authorized access is used to create and manage livestream broadcasts, including titles, descriptions, schedules, and visibility settings.

Credentials and Security

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are intended to be stored in secured application configuration, not published in this website's source code.

Information Sharing

The application is not intended to sell personal information or share authorized YouTube account data with advertisers. Technical service providers may process information necessary to operate the application.

Revoking Access

The account owner can revoke the application's Google Account permissions at: Google Account Connections .
